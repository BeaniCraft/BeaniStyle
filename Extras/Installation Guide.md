## Hit Explosions
Replace "`Explosion.png`" <small>(Located in `"BeaniStyle\Graphics\5_Game\10_Effects\Hit"`)</small> with the one in the `"BeaniStyle\Extras"` folder.

## Good Judgement
Replace "`Judge.png/Judge_ja.png`" <small>(Located in `"BeaniStyle\Graphics\5_Game"`)</small> with the ones in the `"BeaniStyle\Extras"` folder.

## Green KA-DONs
Replace "`Swap.png`" <small>(Located in `"BeaniStyle\Graphics\5_Game"`)</small> with the one in the `"BeaniStyle\Extras"` folder.

## Combo Voice Lines
Go to the character you want to add the voice lines to, and drag the `"Combo"` folder into the `"Sounds"` folder.

## Hit Sounds
Drag the `"BeaniStyle"` folder into the `"OpenTaiko\Global\HitSounds"` folder.
